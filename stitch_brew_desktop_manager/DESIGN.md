# Cyber-Electric Design System

## Visual Identity & Narrative
This design system utilizes a high-contrast, dark-mode aesthetic inspired by futuristic "Material Dark" principles. It balances deep neutral surfaces with vibrant, neon-inflected primary and secondary accents. The atmosphere is professional, sleek, and focused, optimized for low-light environments and technical interfaces.

## Color Palette
The color system is built for performance and depth in a dark environment.
- **Primary Color**: #BB86FC – A soft, luminous lavender that serves as the main interactive accent.
- **Secondary Color**: #03DAC6 – A sharp, electric teal for supporting elements and success states.
- **Neutral Base**: #121212 – A deep, matte charcoal that forms the foundation for backgrounds and surfaces.

## Typography
The system relies on a unified typographic voice to ensure maximum readability and a clean, modernist aesthetic.
- **Headline, Body, and Label Font**: Inter – A versatile sans-serif designed for screen readability, providing a neutral and efficient tone across all text roles.

## Shape and Spatial Rhythm
- **Roundedness**: Subtle (Level 1). UI components feature slightly softened edges, providing a professional look that avoids the harshness of 90-degree angles while maintaining a structured, architectural feel.
- **Spacing**: Normal (Level 2). The interface utilizes standard, balanced whitespace. This transition from compact to normal spacing ensures elements have sufficient room to breathe, improving legibility and reducing visual clutter in complex layouts.
